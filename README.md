## About
This repository contains the submitted version of my Master's thesis *Sheaf Cohomology and Obstructions to Global Lifts*.

The thesis develops foundational aspects of sheaf cohomology, with particular emphasis on the categorical and homological methods underlying its construction and computation. It also contains extensive background material on category theory, abelian and Grothendieck categories, homological algebra, derived functors, sheaves, Čech cohomology, spectral sequences, and comparison results.

A recurring aim throughout the thesis is to make the constructions explicit and to emphasize the motivation and intuition behind the abstract machinery.

## Topics

The thesis discusses, among other things,

* category theory, limits, and colimits,
* abelian and Grothendieck categories,
* projective and injective objects,
* homological algebra and derived functors,
* presheaves and sheaves,
* Čech cohomology,
* sheaf cohomology,
* spectral sequences,
* comparison results between different cohomology theories,
* and cohomological obstruction problems.

In particular, examples involving the exact sequence

$$
0 \longrightarrow \mu_2
\longrightarrow \mathcal O_X^\times
\xrightarrow{(\cdot)^n}
\mathcal O_X^\times
\longrightarrow 0
$$

are used to study obstructions to the existence of square roots of holomorphic functions.
